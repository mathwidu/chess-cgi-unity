# Presentation

Code here is claimed by the presentation domain. Changing it means updating `domainbook/domains/presentation/` in the same commit, or waiving the commit with a "Skip-Docs: <reason>" trailer. Any file under that folder clears the check: the canvas, the glossary, the changelog, a feature, a decision, or a debt record.

Before you name anything here, call `explain_terms` with the words you are about to use — this context has its own, and they win over the book's. `domainbook/domains/presentation/index.md` holds its canvas. Without MCP, its words are in `domainbook/domains/presentation/glossary.md`.
